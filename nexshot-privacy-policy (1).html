Non-Personal Data — anonymous technical information that cannot identify you individually, such as device type, operating system version, session duration, and app performance metrics.

Personal Data — information that identifies or may reasonably identify you. Any non-personal data linked to personal data is treated as personal data for as long as that linkage exists.

03

Camera & Device Permissions

NexShot requires access to your device's camera to deliver its core functionality. Here is exactly how permissions are used:

📷 Camera Access Required
NexShot uses your device camera exclusively to provide live preview, instant photo capture, digital zoom during preview, and photo format selection (HEIC or JPEG). Photos are saved directly to your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
🔔 Push Notifications Optional
We may store a Firebase Cloud Messaging token to send you optional push notifications. To stop notifications, disable them in your device's Settings or uninstall the App. This token is non-personally identifiable.
Important: NexShot operates using only the native camera capabilities provided by iOS. The App does not enhance, extend, or modify the hardware performance of your device's camera. Features such as digital zoom use the device's built-in capabilities and image quality may vary at higher zoom levels depending on your device.

  • Service providers — with processors (servers, payment, advertising, analytics) who are contractually prohibited from using your data for any purpose beyond the services they provide us.
